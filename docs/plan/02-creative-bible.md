# LUMENBLADE — Second Dawn

Working title; original spiritual-sequel IP. Trademark clearance remains an owner release gate.

## Promise
A luminous, side-on sword-and-spell adventure across a world whose dawn has stopped. A tiny human silhouette against an enormous living landscape. Warm campfires, teal forests, icy blue ridges, brass machinery and violet ruins. No borrowed Swordigo assets.

## Story
Wren, apprentice of Thistlebrook's vanished champion Orrin, wakes to an extinguished sky. The six Dawn Shards have been taken by guardians who believed division would prevent the Hollow Sovereign from returning. Orrin's shadow now wears the crown. Restore the shards, cross Hollowgate and decide whether to bind the dawn to a new champion or return its light to everyone.

## Launch scope
22 connected rooms: Thistlebrook hub plus three rooms each in Whisperwood, Glimmerdeep, Mirelake, Frostfang, Emberforge, Skyreach and Hollowgate. Six regional guardians grant shards and abilities; the seventh region ends in a multi-phase mirror duel. Side caches, shrines, shops, portals, journal, equipment, level choices and two endings.

## Dialogue principles
Short, playable exchanges. Orrin is imperfect, not a flawless murdered mentor. The hub keeper Sela explains the cost of a single champion. Enemy tells communicate without prose. Journal descriptions explain unlocked mechanics.

## Presentation
Code-built original low-poly environments and articulated character rigs; orthographic 2.5D camera. Subtle fog, rim lighting, parallax silhouettes, emissive spells, slash trails and restrained particles. Audio is synthesized, original and starts only after a gesture. Fixed dark presentation is intentional for this game. Reduced-motion option suppresses shake and idle particles.
