## Must ship: three-realm upgrade

### 1. Distinct playable routes, not three longer strips

Change `worlds.js` geometry within the existing horizontal/720-height model; retain checkpoint floors, original critical-route accessibility and entity IDs. No moving-platform system or nested-room framework in this increment.

- **Moonroot:** turn the original canopy climbs into an upper exploration loop and lower safer route meeting near Lantern Hollow. Original `m-si0/m-si1` remain accessible; `m-ch1` retains its cache/reward identity but gains a readable canopy approach. Root/socket landmarks foreshadow restoration.
- **Cinderworks:** build distinct gantry and maintenance approaches using static ledges, lava gaps and existing enemy roles. Route choice changes exposure to sentinels/wisps. Preserve `c-*` discoveries and safe Hourglass Bridge recovery.
- **Spire:** replace repeated shelf composition with observatory terraces and broken-hall sightlines inside the current camera bounds. Existing rain warnings and landing surfaces remain simultaneously visible. Preserve `h-*` progression.

Each realm needs one optional loop, one short puzzle using bolt/jump/dash/guard already available, and one clear shortcut back to a known beacon. Do not add required sigils, caches or NPC hand-ins.

### 2. Differentiated guardians and legible combat

Extend `updateBoss`/existing events, preserving guardian IDs and rewards: Warden uses telegraphed root lanes; Hour-Eater uses staged forge waves/recovery; Veyr uses marked rain and a distinct final-phase pattern. All counters use existing moves. Preserve hold-to-attack, precision guard and earned blade/heart/focus investment. Resolve hit/reward uniqueness and input priorities before hit-stop experiments. No persistent equipment inventory.

### 3. Concrete Lyra narrative and actionable journal

Keep the letter, Aster, three hearts, Veyr, mercy/rekindle and Echo Gauntlet. Expand existing NPC/cache/victory text:

- Mara asks Lyra to discover the garden letters; Rootheart restoration visibly opens a bud and changes her dialogue.
- Tovan's caches reveal stolen family summers; Forgeheart recovery changes the furnace's purpose, not merely its color.
- Orin's denied names prepare Lyra's final answer: deliver possibility, not another compulsory future.

Journal entries derive objective/completion from **existing** rune, cache and boss flags. Optional “Garden Letters,” “Summer Coal” and “Unwritten Names” chapters use existing discoveries, not fabricated inventory or retroactive rewards. Speak-before/after-collection produces consistent dialogue. Returns offer changed NPC testimony and scenery; never require old players to repeat cleared enemies or newly mandatory errands.

### 4. Visual improvement without discarding art

Keep supplied panorama and three realm plates. Improve `client.js`/`realm-v11.js` layering, foreground materials, planted animation/attack recovery, Lyra's scarf/tunic silhouette and telegraph contrast. Distant painted bridges must not read as immediate collision surfaces. Preserve fullscreen art while suppressing stacked welcome/title/prompt overlays. Inspect portrait and landscape: larger readable hero presentation, visible landing hazards, compact HUD, labeled 44px touch targets, no extra permanent action buttons. Reduced-motion/low-quality remain functional.
