# Research dossier — spiritual sequel, not an official sequel

## Evidence
- **FACT:** Touch Foo describes Swordigo as exploration of towns, dungeons and treasures, with XP, weapons and spells; dynamic cave lighting and precise touch controls are explicit selling points. Source: https://touchfoo.com/swordigo (accessed 2026-10-13).
- **FACT:** Swordigo released March 22, 2012; it is a single-player platform-adventure/action RPG. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swordigo.
- **FACT:** The four spells are Magic Bolt, Magic Bomb, Dragon's Grasp and Dimension Rift. Bolt also activates switches; Bomb opens blocked passages. Source: https://swordigo.fandom.com/wiki/Weapons and https://swordigo.fandom.com/wiki/Spell. Community wiki details are not official specifications.
- **FACT:** Touch Foo called Grimvalor a spiritual successor to Swordigo. Source: https://www.facebook.com/touchfoo/posts/hey-swordigo-android-fans-the-wait-is-over-grimvalor-the-spiritual-successor-of-/2145961118823558/.

## Translation, not copying
Keep responsive side-on movement in a dimensional world, sword/projectile interactions, discoverable secrets, light RPG growth and ability-gated exploration. Do not reuse characters, sprites, names, music, maps or dialogue. Do not claim affiliation or a licensed sequel. The repository name is the owner's label, not the game's brand.

## Experience hypotheses to validate
1. A full-viewport playable world will feel more like a console adventure than the previous widget.
2. Layered low-poly landscapes, readable enemy windups and warm shrine light can modernize the presentation without losing the inviting tone.
3. Coyote time, buffered jumps and independent touch pointers reduce frustration.
4. A compact complete campaign is more valuable than a sprawling unfinished map.
5. Combat rewards, equipment decisions, spell puzzles and a real ending make exploration purposeful.

These are design hypotheses, not measured audience findings. No claim of trademark clearance or mobile performance certification is made.
