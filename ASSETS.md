## Alpha.4 original optional-room paintings

Four original AI-generated environment plates are shipped as `chapter-millpond.webp`, `chapter-cooling.webp`, `chapter-rootcellar.webp` and `chapter-archive.webp` (each 1376×768, WebP quality88). They depict irrigation terraces, an indoor cooling chamber, rooted cellar masonry and an enclosed star archive. The mill painting was revised to remove a misleading background plank crossing. These are background artwork only; simulation geometry, live hazards and interaction props remain authoritative. The three main-world plates and supplied panorama are unchanged. All four new files are content-verified members of the offline shell. Original generation files and prompts are retained outside the repository; the runtime does not need them.
